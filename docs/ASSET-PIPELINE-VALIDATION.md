## In-game probe status and acceptance procedure

Runtime acceptance: **pending-manual-run**. No validated native game-session evidence is present.

The shipped `PZWorld_ValidationProbe.lua` records load time, player movement, loaded-square availability, chunk transitions, stalls, and Lua errors to `console.txt`. It is observational and does not mutate map squares. Runtime acceptance requires a new save and all of the following:

1. Find `PZWORLD_VALIDATION begin` and `PZWORLD_VALIDATION complete` in `console.txt`.
2. Traverse at least 20 eight-square chunk boundaries and two 256-square cell boundaries for at least five minutes.
3. Require zero missing-player-square samples, zero probe errors, no native crash, and no sustained streaming stall visible to the player.
4. Inspect an urban junction, diagonal curb, highway, rural road, bridge approach, parking area, and building entrance for collisions and invalid/blank tiles.
5. Copy `docs/runtime-observations.example.json`, replace every pending check with dated notes, then validate both artifacts with `npm run validate-in-game-probe -- <console.txt> --observations <runtime-observations.json>`.
